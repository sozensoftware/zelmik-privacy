
Zelmik ("the app") is a racing game for children aged 5–8, developed by Sözen Software ("we", "us"). We built Zelmik to be a safe place for kids, so it collects as little as possible. This policy explains what that means.

Summary
No accounts, no sign-up, no names, no email addresses, no photos.
No advertising of any kind.
No analytics, no tracking, no third-party advertising or tracking SDKs.
No camera, microphone, location, contacts or photo library access.
Game progress is stored only on your device.
Subscriptions are processed by Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play). We never see your payment details.
Information stored on the device

To let your child continue where they left off, the app saves the following locally on the device:

game progress (unlocked worlds and levels, stars, gems),
kart customization choices and garage items,
settings (sound, music, language).

This data never leaves the device and is not sent to us. You can erase it at any time with Grown-ups Area → Reset progress, or by deleting the app.

Subscriptions and purchases

Zelmik offers an optional auto-renewing subscription ("Zelmik Club"). Purchases are handled entirely by Apple or Google; we never receive your name, Apple ID / Google account, or payment card information.

To check whether a subscription is active and to restore purchases, the app uses RevenueCat (RevenueCat, Inc.), a subscription management service. RevenueCat receives:

a random, anonymous app user ID generated by the app (not linked to your identity),
purchase information provided by the store (product, purchase and expiry dates, transaction identifiers),
basic technical data needed to process the request (e.g. app version, device type, OS version, country/storefront).

This information is used only to provide and verify the subscription. It is not used for advertising, profiling or tracking, and it is not linked to your identity. See RevenueCat's privacy policy: https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy

Children's privacy

Zelmik is designed for children and is listed in the Kids category of the App Store. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. We comply with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA), the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and the Turkish Personal Data Protection Law (KVKK No. 6698).

Purchases, the subscription screen, external links and the parent settings are protected by a parental gate (the parent enters their birth year) so that children cannot reach them on their own.

Third-party links

The only external links in the app (terms of use, this privacy policy, subscription management, support email) are behind the parental gate.

Data retention and deletion
Data on the device: deleted when you use "Reset progress" or uninstall the app.
Anonymous purchase records at RevenueCat: kept as long as needed to provide the subscription and to meet legal/accounting obligations. To request deletion, email us (see below). Include the date of purchase so we can locate the anonymous record; please do not send any other personal information.
Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under GDPR or KVKK), you may have the right to access, correct or delete personal data, or to object to its processing. Because we do not hold personal information that identifies you, most requests can be met by resetting progress or deleting the app. For anything else, contact us.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update this page and the "Last updated" date above. Material changes will also be mentioned in the app's release notes.
